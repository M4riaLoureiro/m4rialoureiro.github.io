Research Intern

CerCo - Centre de Recherche Cerveau et Cognition · Toulouse, France · 6 months

Feb – Jul 2019

Research Trainee

CINTESIS - Center for Health Technology and Services Research · Porto, Portugal · 6 months

My first contact with a working environment was during the 2nd year of my Bachelor's degree.
